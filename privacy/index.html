Privacy Policy

Ninety Revs iPhone app and ninetyrevs.com · Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short: Ninety Revs works without an account. Your rides, routes and settings are stored on your iPhone. The app contains no advertising, no tracking and no analytics or crash-reporting services. Data leaves your iPhone only

  • when the app loads weather and maps (Apple, swisstopo),
  • if you use the optional account to back up activities and sync your rider profile, and
  • when you export or share something yourself.

This policy applies to the Ninety Revs iPhone app and to this website. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data stored on your iPhone

The app stores the following data locally on your iPhone. We have no access to it unless you use the optional account (section 6) or send it to us yourself.

  • Activities: start and end time, time zone, duration, distance, speed, ascent and descent, elevation, the GPS track of outdoor rides (positions with time stamps), heart rate, power and cadence from connected sensors, workout results and the name you give an activity.
  • Ride recovery: while a ride is in progress, a checkpoint so that an interrupted ride can be restored. It is removed once the activity is saved or discarded.
  • Routes and maps: GPX files you import and offline map packages you download.
  • Rider profile and settings: FTP, maximum, threshold and resting heart rate, heart-rate zones, data pages, units and display settings.
  • Sensors: the Bluetooth identifier and name of the sensors and trainers you save, so that the app can reconnect to them.

Heart rate is health-related data. Ninety Revs does not read from or write to Apple Health.

The data stays on your iPhone until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; Apple handles those backups. Optional outdoor diagnostics (section 8) are excluded from the backup.

3. Location and Bluetooth

Location

With your permission (“While Using the App”), the app uses your location to record route, distance and elevation during a ride you have started – also while the screen is locked – to show your position on the map, to show your current elevation and to load the weather (section 4). Your location is not sent to us. It only leaves your iPhone as described in sections 4 and 6.

Bluetooth

With your permission, the app connects to heart-rate monitors, cadence sensors and smart trainers to show live ride data, to control the trainer during workouts and to check trainer compatibility. Sensor readings are processed on your iPhone and stored with the activity.

You can change both permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

4. Weather (Apple WeatherKit)

Wind, temperature, forecast and sunrise/sunset times come from Apple WeatherKit. To load them, the app sends your current location to Apple when your location changes noticeably or after ten minutes at the latest, and when you tap “Refresh weather”. Apple processes this request according to the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Maps

  • Apple Maps: the map is displayed with Apple’s MapKit. To show it, your iPhone loads map data for the visible area from Apple. The Apple Privacy Policy applies.
  • swisstopo: if you choose a swisstopo map, the Veloland or Wanderland overlay, or download an offline map package, the app loads map tiles from the Federal Office of Topography swisstopo (wmts.geo.admin.ch and wms.geo.admin.ch). Each request contains the coordinates of the map tile, which reveal the map area you are viewing or downloading, as well as your IP address and the usual technical information of an internet request. The app does not send an account or any identifier. Downloaded tiles are stored on your iPhone. See the legal information of the Swiss federal administration.

6. Optional account

You can ride without an account. If you sign in under Settings → Account, the following applies.

Sign in with Apple

You sign in with Apple. We receive a user identifier from Apple, your email address (or an Apple relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”) and, on the first sign-in, the name you choose to share. Apple’s handling of your sign-in is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

What is stored in the account

  • Account: user identifier, email address and name (if shared).
  • Rider profile: FTP, maximum, threshold and resting heart rate and your heart-rate zones, so they are available on your other devices.
  • Activity backup: a summary of each backed-up activity (start and end time, time zone, activity and route name, distance, duration, moving time, ascent) and the complete activity file, including the GPS track, the per-second recordings of speed, elevation, heart rate, power and cadence, and workout results. If you recorded the activity with outdoor diagnostics turned on, the file also contains the app version, iPhone model and iOS version.

After you sign in, the app asks whether activities already on your iPhone should be backed up (“Back up all” or “Only new activities”). Activities you save while signed in are backed up automatically. Uploads never happen during a ride. If you delete an activity on your iPhone, its backup is deleted too.

Where the data is stored

The account runs on Supabase (Supabase, Inc., USA), which we use as a processor. The database and files are hosted in the region eu-central-2 (Zurich, Switzerland). Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules on the server limit every request to the signed-in user’s own data. To operate and secure the service, Supabase also processes technical access data such as IP addresses. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

Signing out and deleting the account

Signing out keeps your data in the account and on your iPhone. Under Settings → Account → Delete account you can delete your account at any time. After you confirm with Apple, the app’s Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked and your account, your synced rider profile and your activity backup are permanently deleted. Activities and settings on your iPhone are kept.

7. Import, export and sharing

You can import GPX files you select, and export an activity as a GPX file or a diagnostics package. Exports are handed to the iOS share sheet; the recipient is whatever app or person you choose there. Nothing is shared automatically.

8. Outdoor diagnostics

Under Settings → Diagnostics & development you can turn on “Record outdoor diagnostics” (off by default). For the following outdoor rides the app then also stores detailed GPS readings on your iPhone, including discarded points with exact locations and times. This data is not sent anywhere automatically; you can export it from the activity as a diagnostics package.

9. No analytics, advertising or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. If you have allowed your iPhone to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with crash reports and usage statistics that do not identify you. If you test a beta version with TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and the feedback you send with us.

10. This website

This website consists of static pages without cookies, tracking, analytics or externally loaded fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. Our mailboxes are hosted by Apple (iCloud Mail).

12. Purposes and legal bases

We process personal data only to provide the app’s functions and the account you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), to answer your messages (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and, for the website, to operate it securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Heart-rate data is backed up to your account only if you sign in and choose to back up activities; this is based on your consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by deleting the activities or the account.

13. Recipients and transfers abroad

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Recipients are only the providers named above (Apple, swisstopo, Supabase, GitHub) for the purposes described. Supabase stores account data in Switzerland. Where Apple, Supabase or GitHub process data in other countries, including the USA, this is based on their data protection terms, such as standard contractual clauses.

14. Retention

Data on your iPhone stays there until you delete it. Account data is kept until you delete the activity or the account. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request.

15. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive your data in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Most of this you can do directly in the app (deleting activities or the account, exporting GPX). For everything else, write to privacy@ninetyrevs.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.

16. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the website changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
