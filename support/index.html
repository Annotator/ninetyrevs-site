Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to us – we read every message.

Email: support@ninetyrevs.com

For a problem, please include the app version (Settings → Version), your iPhone model and iOS version, the sensors or trainer involved and what you did before it happened.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Riding, workouts, routes and your activity history work without an account and without an internet connection. The optional account (Settings → Account, Sign in with Apple) backs up your activities and keeps your rider profile on all your devices.

Which sensors and trainers work?

Ninety Revs connects via Bluetooth to standard heart-rate monitors, cadence sensors and smart trainers that support the FTMS standard. You can save one sensor per type under Settings → Sensors; the app reconnects to it automatically.

Why does the app need my location?

To record route, distance and elevation during an outdoor ride – also with the screen locked – to show your position on the map and to load the weather for where you are. The app only asks for access “While Using the App”.

Can I use maps offline?

Yes, for Switzerland and Liechtenstein: for an imported route you can download a swisstopo map package along the route and use it without a connection.

How do I get my rides out of the app?

Open an activity, tap the menu at the top right and choose “Export GPX”. You can then save or share the file with any app.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm with Apple. Your account, activity backup and synced rider profile are permanently deleted; activities and settings on your iPhone stay. Details are in the privacy policy.

GPS distance or elevation looks wrong. What helps you investigate?

Turn on Settings → Diagnostics & development → Record outdoor diagnostics before your next outdoor ride. Afterwards open the activity, choose “Export diagnostics package” and send it to support@ninetyrevs.com. The package contains exact locations and times, so only send it if you are comfortable with that.
